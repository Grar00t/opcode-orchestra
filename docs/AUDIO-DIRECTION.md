# Audio direction and source policy

Reference recordings, speaker references, model weights and generated voice
remain private local inputs/outputs. They are not core build inputs, public
artifacts, or material automatically copied into a master. No celebrity voice
impersonation or borrowed commercial composition is required by this project.
Original or explicitly authorized voice inputs are required for optional TTS.

The earlier artistic direction remains an intention: a D-minor, 115-BPM abstract
hip-hop/jazz-spoken-word arrangement, upright-bass and prepared-piano textures,
dry percussion, syncopation, abrupt silence, trumpet accents and a close baritone
voice. The existing source does not prove that this complete arrangement,
instrument realism, tempo, mix or voice has been realized. These are listening
and composition objectives, not build-gate results.

The core path is NASM source to x86 instructions and device writes. Optional
XTTS is not Assembly-native and does not establish the identity or consent of a
speaker by itself. Keep words intelligible in any later mix and evaluate that
with an actual recording/listener; no audibility result is inferred here.
